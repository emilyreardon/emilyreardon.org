I design, make, and study hybrid digital-analog experiences that help people grow, connect, and participate in the world around them.

Working across the material and the technical, my practice draws from play, storytelling, learning, craft, community collaboration, and public justice. I build experiences and systems that invite participation rather than passive consumption, cultivating agency, belonging, and care.

Grounded in open and ethical tools and processes, my experiments ask how digital technologies might deepen, rather than displace, our relationships with one another and with the worlds we inhabit to build more just and loving futures.
